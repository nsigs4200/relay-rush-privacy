Privacy Policy for Relay Rush

Last updated: August 19, 2026

Relay Rush ("the App") is a mobile arcade game developed by Lily Games Studio ("we," "us," or "our"). This Privacy Policy explains how the App handles information when you use it.

Summary

Relay Rush does not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal information. The App does not use analytics, advertising, or third-party tracking of any kind.

Information We Collect

Relay Rush does not collect any personal data. The App does not require an account, does not connect to the internet to transmit data, and does not use any third-party SDKs for analytics, advertising, or crash reporting.

Data Stored On Your Device

The App saves gameplay progress — such as your unlocked levels, high scores, in-game currency, and settings (music/sound/vibration preferences) — locally on your device using standard Android storage (PlayerPrefs). This data:

  • Never leaves your device
  • Is not transmitted to us or to any third party
  • Is only accessible to the App itself
  • Is deleted automatically if you uninstall the App, or manually if you use the in-app "Reset Progress" option

Permissions

Relay Rush requests the Vibrate permission, used only to provide haptic feedback (vibration) during gameplay — for example, when collecting an item or colliding with an obstacle. This permission is not used to access any other device function or data.

Children's Privacy

Relay Rush is intended for a general audience and is not directed specifically at children. We do not knowingly collect any personal information from anyone, including children under 13, because the App does not collect personal information from any user.

Third-Party Services

The App does not integrate any third-party advertising, analytics, or data-sharing services. If this changes in a future update, this Privacy Policy will be updated accordingly before that update is released.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last updated" date above.
